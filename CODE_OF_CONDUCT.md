# Code of conduct

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free community regardless of experience,
identity, background, or viewpoint. Be constructive, assume good intent, accept
correction, and focus criticism on the work. Harassment, threats, doxxing,
sexualized attention, and discriminatory conduct are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation
when conduct is harmful. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially
and fairly as practical.
